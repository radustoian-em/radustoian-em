# Talent Attraction & Agentic AI Strategy Skill

Use this skill when auditing employer brand visibility across LLMs, architecting talent acquisition funnels for agentic job search, or applying Radu Stoian's *Organic First Talent Attraction Strategy*.

## Strategic Foundations

Traditional recruitment marketing relies on paid programmatic job aggregation and classic search engines. In the Agentic AI era, candidates increasingly discover roles through conversational AI, AI-assisted agents (Google Career Dreamer, ChatGPT Jobs, Perplexity), and natural language evaluation.

The goal is to move from **being found by search engines** to **being recommended by AI**.

---

## 1. AI Brand Health Audit

The AI Brand Health Audit solves the "black box" problem of Generative AI for employer branding:

1. **Benchmark Multi-LLM Perception**: Query models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) with candidate prompt variations across job titles, location, and EVP factors.
2. **Quantify Narrative Alignment**: Evaluate whether the model accurately describes compensation, culture, remote policies, and career growth.
3. **Information Supply Chain Auditing**: Identify where LLMs pull employer reputation data (careers site, Glassdoor, news, official documentation, Schema.org entities).
4. **Iterative Remediation**: Inject clean, structured, verified entity data to steer model narratives toward ground truth.

---

## 2. Organic First Talent Attraction Strategy

A sustainable hierarchy of candidate acquisition channels:

```
[Layer 1] Technical SEO & Schema.org Structured Data
[Layer 2] Direct Google for Jobs Optimization (JobPosting schemas)
[Layer 3] AI / LLM Optimization (GEO/AEO, verified citations)
[Layer 4] Organic Social & Employee Advocacy
[Layer 5] Paid Marketing (targeted gap-filling only)
```

- **Organic Foundations First**: Ensure job postings and careers sites are crawlable, structured, and indexable without paywalls or multi-step redirects.
- **Paid Efficiency**: Paid advertising is reserved for immediate surge hiring or hyper-niche academic/executive roles, rather than acting as a perpetual crutch for poor organic visibility.

---

## 3. Next-Gen Careers Sites & Job Hubs

- **Dynamic Job Hubs**: Automate localized and skill-based landing pages capturing long-tail candidate queries.
- **Interactive Conversational Assistants**: Integrate conversational interfaces that help candidates find relevant roles near them based on skills rather than rigid keyword matching.
- **OJCP Compliance**: Ensure job posting URLs follow the Open Job Context Protocol standards (`official_job_url`) so agents route candidates directly to verified employer application systems.
