I provide the strategic leadership and digital infrastructure that help employers capitalise on the shift to Agentic AI. From building a proprietary AI Brand Health Audit product to deploying smart careers sites, I deliver results that help employers attract the talent they need to drive their businesses into the future.

My goal is simple: to help employers move from being ‘found’ by search engines to being ‘recommended’ by AI.

My approach centres on an Organic First Talent Attraction Strategy, anticipating the shift in user behaviour towards AI-driven job discovery. By optimising for Google's "Career Dreamer" intent, I ensure organisations aren't just broadly visible, but contextually and deeply relevant across the entire candidate journey.

My expertise spans AI, SEO, Performance Marketing, Web Development, and Google Cloud. This technical breadth lets me build data-driven strategies backed by a deep understanding of how digital marketing and user behaviour intersect.

Clients and colleagues know me as someone who won’t take “it can’t be done” as an answer. If there’s a way to improve performance, I’ll find it. If there isn’t an established way to do something, I’ll innovate.
