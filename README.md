# [Radu Stoian](https://www.linkedin.com/in/radustoian/)

Technical Director at [Enhance Media](https://enhancemedia.co.uk/ai).

My goal is simple: To help employers move from being **'found'** by search engines to being **'recommended'** by AI. 

I provide the strategic leadership and digital infrastructure that helps employers capitalise on the shift to Agentic AI — from building proprietary AI Brand Health Audit tools to deploying smart, high-converting careers sites.

---

## 🚀 Key Areas of Focus

* **Pioneering the Agentic AI Era** — Built and launched the *AI Brand Health Audit*, a solution designed to solve the 'black box' problem of Generative AI by helping organizations measure, benchmark, and control their brand narrative in LLMs.
* **Digital Strategy & Technical SEO** — Crafting data-driven strategies built on robust technical SEO foundations to drive measurable performance.
* **Next-Gen Careers Sites** — Leading the deployment of smart careers sites featuring advanced job detail pages and automated Job Hubs that personalize candidate journeys and capture long-tail search traffic.
* **Mentorship & Advocacy** — Upskilling the next generation of digital leaders and sharing knowledge through industry masterclasses (including BrightonSEO).
